About

I've been working in software since 2016, and I run two ventures — InstrumentGuru and Chairy Foundation — where I handle software design, solutioning, support, and upgrades for clients end to end.

My background sits at the intersection of IT and industrial systems: an MBA in IT and Administration, a bachelor's degree in Computer Science, and hands-on time with control & instrumentation — PLC and DCS software included. I'm an ex-TCS employee and an ex-KNITian (Kamla Nehru Institute of Technology), where I was also a member of the KNIT Developer Club.

I keep learning deliberately — a steady stream of Udemy courses to stay current — and I like taking on work across the stack: databases, Python/Django/Node.js backends, Android and desktop apps, embedded and modern IoT development, and the Kafka/MQTT infrastructure that ties it together.

I also go by Vineet Maurya as a personal brand name across some of my earlier work — same person, same GitHub.
